# Privacy

Veylo's private payouts are confidential USDC payouts on NEAR.

**Private means no public source we checked links a confidential payout to the company, provided the payout address has never received a public payment from it.**

What that does and doesn't mean:

- It's what we checked on mainnet (see [docs/EVIDENCE.md](docs/EVIDENCE.md)), not a guarantee of anonymity.
- If the payout address has received a public payment from the company before, the two can be linked.
- The contractor, Veylo, and the settlement service (1Click and the confidential ledger's operators) can still see payout details.
- Direct payouts are public: anyone can see them on the NEAR explorer.

Veylo keeps the data needed to run payroll (contractors, payout addresses, pay runs, payslips) and never sells it. Your session is a secure cookie; the app stores only your theme and organisation choice in your browser.
