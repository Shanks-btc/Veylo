Questions

Common questions

Do my contractors need crypto experience?

They add one payout address. Private payouts go to a NEAR account; direct payouts go to a NEAR account too. They never need gas.

Is private the same as anonymous?

No. No public source we checked links a confidential payout to your company, provided the payout address has never received a public payment from you.

Who holds the money?

Your treasury account. Nothing moves unless your treasury wallet signs it.

What does it cost?

Each payout carries a route fee, shown from the signed quote before you sign. [Veylo pricing to be published.]
